# Vision

## Why?

**The Todo App** is an attempt to build the productivity app I always wanted.

I got tired of subscriptions for basic features, limited customization, and the lack of proper Linux support in many mainstream productivity apps.

This project exists to prove that a modern productivity app can be powerful, beautiful, fully offline, and open source.

## Goals

The Todo App aims to be:

- Offline-first
- Free and open source
- Highly customizable
- Cross-platform (Android, Linux and Web)
- Fast and responsive
- Accessible
- Respectful of user privacy

## Philosophy

The application should always prioritize user freedom.

Features should be optional whenever possible, allowing users to build the experience that works best for them.

The app should never require an internet connection or an account to be useful.

The Todo App is developed by people who value digital freedom. As a result, our primary development targets are Android and Linux.
A fully featured Web client will also be available, making the app accessible on any modern operating system.
Since The Todo App is free and open source, anyone is welcome to contribute or maintain support for additional platforms, including Windows.

## What this project is NOT

The Todo App is **not** intended to become another all-in-one workspace.

The focus is productivity, not collaboration.

The app should remain simple enough to use every day while still offering deep customization for users who want it.
